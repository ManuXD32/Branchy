# Reproducing the results

The article (`branchy-article.pdf`) describes the experiments. The scripts in `scripts/` are the same measurements with your worker names.

Speeds depend on your devices and your network. What should reproduce is the pattern: which arrangement is faster
than which, and by roughly how much.

| Result in the article                                              | How to run it                                                                                                                                                                      | Our numbers                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Branch split with delayed execution removes the network wait (4.6) | `bash scripts/bench_branch_split.sh laptop phone`                                                                                                                                  | 3 devices 13.8 to 16.2 tokens/s; server alone 7.6; waiting 0.1 ms per token                                                                                       |
| A worker as coordinator (4.6)                                      | `remote_coord.py`, see `SETUP.md` section 5                                                                                                                                        | laptop + phone 11.0; phone + laptop 13.3                                                                                                                          |
| Layer split, the llama.cpp RPC layout (4.7)                        | `bash scripts/bench_layer_split.sh laptop phone`                                                                                                                                   | about half the speed of the coordinator alone                                                                                                                     |
| Devices with too little RAM, real devices (4.9)                    | `bash scripts/bench_low_memory.sh devices laptop phone 800`                                                                                                                        | laptop alone 8.9 → 0.67 tokens/s, phone alone 23.9 → 0.65; both together with the budget 6.9 to 7.1 (layer split) and 8.9 to 11.0 (branch split) over a 46 ms VPN |
| Devices with too little RAM, simulated (4.8)                       | `sudo -E bash scripts/bench_low_memory.sh simulated` (Linux, systemd, cgroup v2)                                                                                                   | one device 8.1 → 0.9 tokens/s; two devices 6.0 (layer split) and 9.2 (branch split) at 20 ms                                                                      |
| Everything interactively                                           | `bash scripts/start_webui.sh`                                                                                                                                                      |                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Quality of the two models (4.2)                                    | `python src/quality/quality_bench.py --help` (needs TinyStories and BLiMP), `judge_stories.py` for the blind comparison with a judge model behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint    | loss 1.143 against 1.255                                                                                                                                          |
| Rounding the weights (4.4)                                         | `python src/quality/quant_check.py --help`                                                                                                                                         | 4 bits on the branches: +0.001 loss                                                                                                                               |
| Training the two models (4.2)                                      | `python src/tools/prepare_tinystories_full.py`, then `python src/proxy/train_300m.py --name seq-full --sequential ...` and `--name branched --branches 8 --drop 0.2 --delay 1 ...` | about 20 hours each on an RTX 4000 Ada                                                                                                                            |
| Small-model architecture ranking (4.1)                             | `python src/proxy/train_proxy.py --help`                                                                                                                                           | article                                                                                                                                                           |
| Pretrained models reshaped into branches (4.5, 4.7)                | `src/pretrained/` (needs `transformers` and the model weights)                                                                                                                     | article                                                                                                                                                           |

Every placement benchmark checks itself: a split must reproduce the logits of the single-device run within 2e-3
(we saw 1e-5), otherwise it stops.
